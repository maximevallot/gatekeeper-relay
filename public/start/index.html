Download Gatekeeper

For Chrome or Brave on a computer · 0.2.0 · 110 KB

  1. Open the download (on Windows, choose Extract all). Move the Gatekeeper folder to Documents and leave it there.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions (in Brave, brave://extensions). Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the Gatekeeper folder.
  4. Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin Gatekeeper. Its welcome page opens on its own.

What to expect

  • The first week it only watches. Browse as you normally would.
  • Then you set your limits, or tell it what you want in a sentence.
  • Use one browser for now.
